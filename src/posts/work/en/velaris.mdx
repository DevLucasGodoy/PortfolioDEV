## Overview

**Velaris** is a CRM and management platform for agencies, consultancies and technology companies, covering sales through finance in a single interface.

_"From first contact to signed deal."_

## Problem

Agencies usually run on separate tools for sales, projects and finance. That creates rework, poor visibility and decisions based on stale data.

## Solution

- **Clients and sales pipeline**
- **Projects** in Kanban, sold or internal
- **Tasks and tickets** with SLA
- **Finance** with chart of accounts, P&L and commissions
- **Recurring contracts** with 30, 15 and 7-day alerts
- **Automatic emails**, Google Calendar and PWA

## Tech Stack

- **App**: React 19, Vite 6, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4
- **Data and auth**: Neon (PostgreSQL)
- **API**: Express, Resend, Google Gemini for reports
- **Site**: Next.js 15 on Vercel
- **Quality**: Vitest and per-project CI
