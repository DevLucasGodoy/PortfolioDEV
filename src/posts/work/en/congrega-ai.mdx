## Overview

**Congrega Aí** brings a congregation's schedule, assignments, announcements and field reports into one app, from secretary to publisher. An independent, unofficial project, currently in beta.

## Problem

Organization tends to be scattered across paper, spreadsheets and message groups, and people with an assignment don't always hear about it in time.

## Solution

- **Meeting schedules** and assignments on the phone
- **Announcements and events**
- **Field reports** and duties
- **Territories**
- **Automatic reminders**
- **Three languages**: Portuguese, Spanish and English

## Tech Stack

- **App**: Next.js 16, React 19, next-intl, PWA with Serwist
- **Data**: Neon, Drizzle ORM, Vercel Blob
- **Auth**: Better Auth, with data isolated per congregation
- **Tests**: Vitest and Playwright

## By the numbers

51 tables and about 350 test cases across 65 files.
